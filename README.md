Open-world crime on the Leonida coast, built for the
[Make GTA 6](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) bounty on make.fun.

Drive a Lamborghini down the beachfront with a tank on your tail and a
helicopter overhead, fight on foot through the packed Lantern Market with
armed crews, and watch the whole career — map, stats, workshop — on the
meta screen. Three chapters, a five-star wanted system, crew abilities,
and a city that never stops moving.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in a browser (or serve the `game/` folder from any
static server) and press Space. No build step, no network: the 3D engine
is vendored in `game/three.module.min.js`.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Drive / steer / walk |
| Space | Brake |
| E | Enter or exit the car |
| F | Take down a rival up close, on foot |
| M or Tab | Map / stats / workshop screen |
| Z / X / C | Crew abilities — swap plates, quick fix, eyes out |
| P | Pause |

## Missions

1. **MARKET SQUEEZE** — drive to the Lantern Market, take out three
   rivals on foot in the crowd, reach the safehouse.
2. **WRECK THE REGENT** — ram the Regent three times on the beachfront
   while a tank and six SBP units close in, then lose the heat.
3. **NEON TIDE** — outrun the first wave through Central at night and
   vanish into Old Town.
